Remarks

The M5PM1 handles the battery and USB power paths, charging control and status, board power gates and external 5V switching. This binding exposes the telemetry and control features that are most commonly used (battery/VBUS/5V-out voltage, external 5V enable, charge enable, charging status and the power source). The register behaviour is ported from the M5Stack M5Unified power implementation, which is the most authoritative reference for the M5PM1.
